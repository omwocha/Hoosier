About APNAD

A ministry built on shared calling

From the small congregations of the Canadian prairies to the multicultural megachurches of the Pacific coast, the pastors of the North American Division share one calling — to shepherd Christ's people faithfully until He returns.

APNAD exists to make sure no pastor walks that calling alone. We host the annual convention, publish the quarterly journal Shepherding Together, run the Member Care Hotline, and partner with the NAD Ministerial Department on ordination, mentoring, and clergy wellness.

— The APNAD Executive Team
